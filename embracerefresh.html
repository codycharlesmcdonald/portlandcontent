The course

  • Your lessons, re-edited

    We recut all 17 lessons from your original footage. Each one gets a tighter edit around your key adjustments, color, cleaned-up sound, licensed music, captions, the new title look, and your finished images brought up full frame beside the moment that made them.

  • Your lessons, filmed again

    We film the whole course again in your studio: the welcome, every Booty, Midsection, and Arms pose, and The Senses, with you teaching to camera, directing the model, and showing what your camera sees. Each posing lesson gets the same shape, from your finished image as the open through the setup, your exact direction words, and the common mistakes, with short moments from the model on how each pose feels from her side of the camera. The Senses opens up to the studio itself, so sight, smell, sound, taste, touch, and your playlist each start with how you set the room before a client walks in.

  • The Light

    A new module of three lessons on your off-camera flash setup, direct light, and how the angle of the light softens dimples, filmed during the lesson shoot with the same model.

Around the course

  • Studio overview film

    A two to three minute film about Peekaboo for your website and social, with a vertical version, built around your own story of why you started the studio, what you want a client to feel walking in, and why you teach.

  • Course trailer and promos

    A 60 to 90 second trailer for your sales page and the podcast, plus vertical cutdowns for Instagram and TikTok.

  • The Posing Deck, refreshed

    Our design team at Portland Content redesigns your Posing Deck in the new look of the course, with a reference page for every pose.
