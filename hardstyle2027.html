Ideas for the year

  1. 1

    A new homepage loop

    The video at the top of your homepage is a 15 minute file that goes dark after the first minute. We'd swap in a short loop of a class in session that loads fast.

  2. 2

    Your first class

    We'd make a short film that walks a new member through Foundations and the six kettlebell basics, for the intro 4-pack. It can sit on your classes page and run in ads.

  3. 3

    Coach shorts

    Each of your coaches gets a short vertical built from the story in their bio, like Teal finding kettlebells after twelve ankle surgeries.

  4. 4

    Member stories

    We'd film three members telling their own story with real sound and light, to take the place of the phone testimonials on your site and YouTube.

  5. 5

    Your events

    We'd film a half day at three of your events, like a strength challenge or a certification, and cut a recap from each one.

  6. 6

    Technique shorts

    We'd cut two short verticals a month on one lift each, from the swing to the get-up, so your feed keeps moving between events.
