Services

Every plan starts with a content retainer tailored to your team and workflow, built for channel specific deliverables and clear reporting. Management lines are here when you're ready. Each one has its own price, quoted for your scope, and its own place in the monthly report. Every call is free.

01

Content retainers

The base of every plan.

What you get

A visit on a set day, concepts batched from your list, and each piece cut for where it's going. That might be a vertical clip with captions for the feed, or a wide cut for the site when a piece deserves one.

How it runs

You keep a concept list and we work from it. Clips land in batches you can schedule a month out. Once a month we go through what performed and set the next list.

In the report

What posted, what got finished, what got saved and sent on.

02

Social management

Posting, replies and the calendar.

What you get

The calendar, posting on a schedule we set together, and replies handled in your voice.

How it runs

You see the calendar before anything posts. Changes go in the same day you ask.

In the report

One page a month: reach, follows, replies, and what we're changing next.

03

Paid media

Ads from the footage we already shot.

What you get

Ads built from the same footage, aimed at a zip code and a time of day. Social ads first, since that's where the clips already live, and streaming or search when they fit. Setup once, then month to month management with one creative swap a quarter.

How it runs

The ad accounts are in your name and the spend stays on your card.

In the report

Reach, completion rate, frequency, and site visits when a pixel is on.

04

Web and brand

Identity, site design and site care, in house.

What you get

Identity, site design and monthly site care, with one contact on our side.

How it runs

Quoted per project for identity and design, monthly for site care.

In the report

Site visits and the pages that drive the inquiries.

05

Listings and local search

Your listing, kept current.

What you get

Your business listing cleaned up and kept worth clicking: categories, hours, photos and events right, across every location.

How it runs

A one time cleanup, then a monthly pass with the new photos and events from the shoots.

In the report

Profile views, direction requests and calls from the listing.

06

Incentive programs

Films for corporate incentive trips.

What you get

Story driven films, nightly drops for your channels and photo coverage of every function, edited on site. The recap plays on the final night of the program.

How it runs

A team of two or three with current passports and gear that flies as luggage. We work from your agenda, and one person stays your contact from preproduction through delivery.

In the report

What played on site, what posted during the program, and what went out after.
