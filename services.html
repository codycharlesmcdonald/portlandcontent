Services

Every plan starts with a content retainer, because that's where the footage comes from. The rest are lines you switch on when you're ready. Each one has its own price, quoted for your scope, and its own place in the monthly report. Every call is free.

01

Content retainers

The base of every plan.

What you get

A visit on a set day, two to four concepts batched from your list, and the clips cut vertical with captions and hooks. A sixteen by nine cut when a piece deserves one.

How it runs

You keep a concept list and we work from it. Clips land in batches you can schedule a month out. Once a month we go through what performed and set the next list.

In the report

What posted, what got finished, what got saved and sent on.

02

Social management

Posting, replies and the calendar.

What you get

The calendar, posting Tuesday through Friday, replies and DMs handled in your voice.

How it runs

You see the calendar before anything posts. Changes go in the same day you ask.

In the report

One page a month: reach, follows, replies, and what we're changing next.

03

Paid media

Meta, Google, YouTube and Roku.

What you get

Ads built from the same footage, aimed at a zip code and a time of day. Setup once, then month to month management with one creative swap a quarter.

How it runs

The ad accounts are in your name and the spend stays on your card. We're a user on the account, never the payer.

In the report

Reach, completion rate, frequency, and site visits when a pixel is on.

04

Email

Member and event sends.

What you get

Member and event emails written and scheduled, with the automations that keep a list warm between shoots.

How it runs

We write from your calendar and your list. You approve the send before it goes.

In the report

Opens, clicks, and what each send brought in.

05

Web and brand

Through our design partner.

What you get

Identity, site design and monthly site care, with one contact on our side.

How it runs

Quoted per project for identity and design, monthly for site care.

In the report

Site visits and the pages that drive the inquiries.

06

Listings and local search

Your Google Business Profile.

What you get

The profile cleaned up and kept current: categories, hours, photos and events, across every location.

How it runs

A one time cleanup, then a monthly pass with the new photos and events from the shoots.

In the report

Profile views, direction requests and calls from the listing.

07

Incentive programs

Films for corporate incentive trips.

What you get

Story driven films, nightly drops for your channels and photo coverage of every function, edited on site. The recap plays on the final night of the program.

How it runs

A team of two or three with current passports and gear that flies as luggage. We work from your agenda, and one person stays your contact from preproduction through delivery.

In the report

What played on site, what posted during the program, and what went out after.
