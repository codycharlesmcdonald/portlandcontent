Prepared for LucidWild Estate, November 2026

A content plan for LucidWild Estate

Your posting asked for a part time content creator and videographer. This page shows how we would fill that role on a monthly retainer, with the same weekly rhythm and a film crew behind it.

Book a time to talk
01

We already know the estate

We filmed Vitaliy and Lilia's wedding on the estate in August with J29 Events, so we already know the estate and how it looks on camera. The first visit can go straight to work.

02

A weekly visit, built from your list

Once a week we come out for a short visit and batch two to four ideas from your list. Each clip is cut vertical, with captions and a strong opening frame, ready for the days you post. Once a month we sit down with you and look at what performed, then set the next list.

  • 01

    One visit a week

    On a day the tasting room is open.

  • 02

    A full kit every time

    Cinema cameras, audio, lighting and a licensed drone on every visit.

  • 03

    A monthly check-in

    We look at what worked and set the next list together.

03

Every event is a shoot

Your calendar already does the hard part. Each event gets a recap for your Previous Event Videos page and short cuts for the feed the next morning, so the next invitation has something to show.

  • Après Ski PartySunday, December 6
  • Members' nights
  • Wine dinners and club parties
  • Private and corporate events

Events are quoted one at a time, so you only book the nights that matter.

04

The wines, the club and the vineyard

Between events, the library fills with the estate itself, from a short piece for each wine as it releases to the vineyard through the seasons, and the club sees each film first.

05

Your channels, when you want them run

If you'd rather hand off the posting too, we can run your calendar, post on a schedule you can see, answer comments in the estate's voice, and send you one page a month on what moved. The wine club email and your Google listing can come along when you're ready. We'd scope those with you on a call.

Talk it through

We can start with the next visit and the Après Ski Party on December 6, and add the rest when it makes sense for you.
