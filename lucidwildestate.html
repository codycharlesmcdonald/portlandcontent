A content plan for LucidWild Estate

Your posting asked for a part time content creator and videographer. This page shows how we'd fill that role on a monthly retainer, with the same weekly rhythm and a film crew behind it. Tick what fits, and your plan comes with you to the call.

The role

Weekly content visits

Once a week we come out for a short visit, film two to four ideas from your list, and edit them for the days you post. Once a month we sit down with you, look at what performed, and set the next list.

  • One visit a weekWe come on a day the tasting room is open.
  • A full kit every timeWe bring cinema cameras, audio, lighting, and a licensed drone.
  • A monthly check-inWe look at what worked and set the next list.
The role

Event films

Your calendar already does the hard part. Each event gets a recap for your Previous Event Videos page and short cuts for the feed, so the next invitation has something to show. Events are quoted one at a time, so you only book the nights that matter.

  • Après Ski PartySunday, December 6
  • Members' nightsWeekly
  • Wine dinners and club partiesAs booked
  • Private and corporate eventsAs booked

Venue film for weddings and private events

Couples and companies book the estate after they picture their day there. A short venue film of the grounds and the event spaces, shot on a quiet day with no guests in it, gives your events team something to send with every inquiry and a film for the weddings and private events pages.

Your channels

Social media management

If you'd rather hand off the posting too, we can run your calendar and post on a schedule you can see, answering comments in the estate's voice. Once a month we let you know what moved.

Your channels

Google listing and reviews

Guests look up the tasting room hours and events on Google before they drive out to Dundee. We keep your listing current with fresh photos and your event dates, and make it easy for guests to leave a review after a visit.
