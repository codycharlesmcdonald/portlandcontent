A banner loop, a vertical event film, and an overview for one Portland gym.

Hardstyle Strength trains kettlebell and barbell technique at SE 37th and Powell. We filmed the gym in April, May, and July, and cut each piece for the place it would run.

A year of content for Hardstyle Strength

Frame from the April banner loop, shot during a class.

Hardstyle Strength is a small gym in Southeast Portland, run by a StrongFirst certified instructor who coaches kettlebell and barbell technique for strength that lasts.

The films

How it came together

Our crew filmed the gym three times in four months, once for a website banner, once for a film ahead of the barbell certification, and once for an overview of the classes. Because the same crew shot all three, the gym looks and sounds like itself in every piece.

“This gym was created basically out of my own journey.”The owner, in the barbell certification film

What the gym got

  • A silent banner loop for the top of the website.
  • A vertical film ahead of the barbell certification.
  • A four by five overview of the gym and its classes for the feed.
