Why it works

People are tired of bad content. Our studio, Mac Films, has been producing content for broadcast, commercial, and streaming services for over a decade, and scoping social assets for each production. Portland Content came out of the studio's longtime clients wanting an easy way to get their content into action without pulling them away from their jobs. People finish work that looks good, and platforms reward that with reach.

01

We film with intention

Our sets are built around a premium asset that feeds a library of marketing content. A crew sized to the job, led by a cinematography crew good enough for Intel, Vox and Keurig Dr Pepper. Whatever we're filming, it gets the treatment a brand film gets, and you can see it in the energy of every clip.

02

Social content people finish

Every clip opens on the strongest frame, carries captions for people watching with the sound off, and ends before it drags. Completion is the number platforms pay for, and it's the number we cut for.

03

Take your channels off your plate

We get your content out into the world, make sure your replies are handled and your ads are aimed at the people near you, and let you know what moved and what we're changing next.

04

Have the same team on set as on your call

The person who takes your call comes on set and reviews the edits. We're an intimate team built for our home market, the kind that skips the booking link and gives you a cell phone number.
