Start here

Horizontal re-edits for web

In September 2025 we made four short films with you, cut vertical for Instagram. We can re-cut each one horizontally for pdxrob.com, so the home page and the About page open with you on camera, talking about how you work. The footage is already in our archive, which keeps the re-cuts quick.

Why it mattersThese four films already exist, so they're the fastest way to put you on camera on the new site.

Your listings

Monthly content retainer

The retainer starts with your listings, because each one already has a date to work toward. Every month we film what you have on the market and cut it for the listing page and for your feed. As the library grows, it takes in your flips from purchase to sale.

Why it mattersListings already have dates, so they give the retainer a steady rhythm from the first month, and every shoot feeds the site and your channels at once.

Your listings

Listing walkthrough films

One visit gives you a horizontal walkthrough for the listing and the site, plus vertical cuts for social. Laurelhurst, Hayden Island, and Martha St. are the ones we have already made for you.

Why it mattersSellers choose an agent partly on how their home will be shown. A walkthrough on every listing is something you can promise at the listing appointment.

Your listings

On-camera social series

Short pieces with you talking about the market and about owning rentals, in the format of the interview series we made this summer. They keep you in front of buyers and investors between listings.

Why it mattersPeople hire the agent they feel they already know. Regular pieces with you on camera build that before the first call.

Your listings

Flip case studies

We film the house the week you buy it and again when the work is done, with short check-ins in between that keep the feed moving. Each flip ends up as a case study for the site.

Why it mattersYour contractor side is what sets you apart, and a before and after shows it faster than a list of services.

Cascadia

Property films for Cascadia

The people who invest with Cascadia put money into houses they may never walk through. Your Past Purchases map already marks the houses, and each one could open into a short film and a page, starting with Corbett, which we have already filmed. The same visit gives Real Estate Rob a case study, and if the house becomes a rental, Better Home Solutions has the listing film ready.

Why it mattersOne visit serves all three companies, which makes this the most efficient shoot on the list.
